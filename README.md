# Chattogram Drainage and Canal Renewal: Research Meets Real Action

> Chattogram never stops working, and neither do the people who keep its streets dry.

A short project note that connects my urban planning research on rainwater movement in the Chattogram City Corporation (CCC) area with the drainage and canal work now under way in the city. It is based on a LinkedIn post, and this repository holds the written summary only. It contains no thesis data or map files.

## The research side

As an urban planner, I studied how rainwater moves through the CCC area using ArcGIS Pro:

- SRTM elevation data
- Watershed delineation
- Isochrone (travel-time) zones
- Unit hydrographs for five watersheds: Kalurghat, Bakalia, Chittagong Port, Kattali-1 and Kattali-2

The work was a joint undergraduate thesis with Uzzal Hossen at CUET. Full summary: [chattogram-flood-prediction](https://github.com/faridur1995/chattogram-flood-prediction).

**One lesson that stood out:** small, dense watersheds such as Bakalia and the Port area reach peak flow within minutes of rain, so well-maintained drainage there pays off immediately.

## The action side

The following items are as described in my LinkedIn post. Figures come from public reporting and have not been independently re-checked here, so please confirm them with the official sources before reusing them.

| Item | What the post reports |
| --- | --- |
| CDA canal renovation | A mega project to renovate 36 canals, about 95% complete as of early 2026, with 163 km of retaining walls, 115 bridges and culverts, and 27 silt traps |
| Bangladesh Army, 34 Engineer Construction Brigade | Construction in difficult weather and crowded neighbourhoods |
| Handover and next steps | A handover plan with training for City Corporation staff, and plans to recover 21 more canals |
| CCC field teams | Pre-monsoon drain cleaning and round-the-clock monitoring |

## Where GIS can add more

GIS, drone mapping and Web GIS dashboards can make this progress stronger, for example by tracking canal condition, silt build-up and waterlogging hotspots in one place.
